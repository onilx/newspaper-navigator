New directories available with 10,000 more entries

By MERYL NELSON
Staff Writer
The University publishes The
Directory of Students each year to
help students keep in touch during
vacations and locate each other on
campus during the school year.
The 1980-81 edition of the direc-
tory contains more than 25,000
listings, 10,000 more names than
last year, according to Sherwin
Horowitz of the Emanon
Publishing Company, the
publisher of the directory.
The listings include home and

campus addresses and phone
numbers of undergraduate and
graduate students.
The directory also contains
departmental listing for the New
Brunswick, Camden, and Newark
campuses.
The yellow pages of the direc-
tory includes discount coupons
redeemable at local shops and
eateries.

The directory is now available at
all Un versity bookstores for
$1.50.

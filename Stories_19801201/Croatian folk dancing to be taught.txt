Croatian folk dancing to be taught

George Tomov, an authority on
Yugoslav dance, music and folk
culture, will demonstrate and
teach Croatian folk dances on
Wednesday, Dec. 3.
The program is sponsored by the
Rutgers Folk Dancers, a
recreational folk dancing group
that meets weekly at the Univer-
sity.
Open to the public, the
workshop on Croatian dance will
be held from 7 to 10:30 p.m. at the
Ledge, the commuter students'
center on George Street. A dona-
tion will be requested.
Tomov maintains his own dance
studio in Manhattan and is artistic
director and choreographer of the
Tomov Ensemble, a Yugoslav folk
dance troupe. He travels widely to
lecture and give master classes and
workshops.
The Rutgers Folk Dancers
usually meet on Wednesdays from
7-10 p.m. at Bartlett Hall on
Lipman Drive at Cook College.
Membership is open to all in-
terested persons, whether or not
they are affiliated with Rutgers.
The group dances to live music.

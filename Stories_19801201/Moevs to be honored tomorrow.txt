Moevs to be honored tomorrow

Robert Moevs, Rutgers'
longtime music professor, will be
honored on the occasion of his
60th birthday by both a recital and
a concert of his music tomorrow in
Kirkpatrick Chapel.
The first of the two programs
will be the performance by pianist
Wanda Maximilien of Moevs' con-
temporary 'composition,
"Phoenix," at 12:15 p.m. Max-
imilien is an associate of music at
Rutgers College.

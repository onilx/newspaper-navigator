Fraternity recognizes black achievements

For 69 years, the nationwide
Omega Psi Phi fraternity has been
aimed at social service, and the
University's Tau Zeta chapter
recently honored several black
groups and individuals at Rutgers
as part of its Achievement Week.
During the sixth annual
Achievement Week, the fraternity
presented certificates and plaques
to Tyree McQueen, a Livingston
College senior who was selected as
the outstanding undergraduate
Omega brother and scholar.
Scarlet Knights' quarterback
Eddie McMichael received an
award as outstanding
athlete/scholar, and Herb
Thompson was named Omega
Man of the Year. The fraternity
awarded Livingston senior Peter
McKnight as scholar of the year
and chose Noah Hart, assistant
dean at Livingston, as outstanding
among the faculty/administration.
The Omega commended the 10-
year-old Black Voice/Carta
Boricua as this year's outstanding
social organization, presenting the
newspaper with a plaque tht now
hangs on the wall of its Rutgers
Student Center office.
Tanya Davis, Black Voice/-
Carta Boricua editor-in-chief, said
the award will not only prove a
"good incentive" to members of
the newspaper, but it signifies
more recognition of the publica-
tion in black community.
Tha award will make "future
staff members be proud of the
Black Voice and want to strive
towards excelling. All students are
starting to realize how important
we are," said the Rutgers College
senior.
Achievement Week was fol-
lowed by Founders Week, when
Omega Psi Phi celebrated its 69th
anniversary as a national frater-
nity. Included in the week's events
were exhibits, movies featuring
black individuals and their
achievements, awards and a talent
show in which Rutgers' Tau Zeta
chapter garnered first place.
The fraternity this year spon-
sored two Halloween parties at the
Livingston Avenue YMCA in New
Brunswick and the Hamilton
Street Afterschool Program in
Somerset to benefit local children.
The social service fraternity is
currently conducting a food and
clothing drive that will end Thurs-
day. Donations, which can be
dropped off today and Thursday at
any student center, will go to the
Children's Aid and Adoption
Society.

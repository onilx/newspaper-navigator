Rogers tops Heisman choices

NEW YORK (AP) - A senior
running back, South Carolina's
George Rogers and a senior
quarterback, Perdue's Mark Her-
rman are among the favorites for
the Heisman Trophy, but a defen-
sive standout Pitt's Hugh Green
and a freshman Georgia tailback
Herschel Walker also are in the
running.
The winner of the 46th Heisman
Trophy, awarded annually by the
Downtown Athletic Club of New
York to what it terms the nation's
outstanding college football
player, will be announced today.
Rogers is believed to have a

slight edge after leading the
country in rushing with 1,781
yards, an average of 161.9 per
game. He has rushed for more
than 100 yards in 20 consecutive
games.
The Heisman balloting closed
Friday, prior to the final regular-
season games of Green, Walker,
Brigham Young quarterback Jim
. McMahon and tailbacks Freeman
McNeil of UCLA and Marcus Al-
len of Southern California.
"As I've said many times, I wish
they'd wait until after the last
game to vote on the Heisman
Trophy," Coach Vince Dooley

said Saturday after Walker rushed
for 205 yards and three
touchdowns in No. 1 ranked
Georgia's 38-20 victory over
Georgia Tech, breaking the
freshman mark of Pitt's Tony
Dorsett 1,586 yards in 1973.
Walker, who didn't start
Georgia's opener and was slowed
by an ankle injury in two other
games, finished fourth nationally
with 1,616 yards and 146.9-yard
average. He averaged 5.9 yards per
carry to Rogers' 6.0.
Herrmann finished second
nationally in total offense and fifth
in passing but set career NCAA
records for pass yardage 9,188,
completions 717 and attempts
1,218. During the 1980 season, he
completed 220 of 340 attempts for
2,923 yards and 19 touchdowns,
while missing one game.
Jim McMahon of Brigham
Young, a junior who led the
country in total offense and pass-
ing, is expected to finish high in the
voting. For the regular season, he
was 284 of 445 and set a pair of
single-season NCAA marks by
throwing for 4,571 yards and 47
touchdowns.

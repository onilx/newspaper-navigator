What Reagan will do for us

To the Editor:
Uncle Sam's robots will march
gloriously in red, white and blue as
the blacks in New Brunswick sit
silently on the doorsteps of un-
employment. As Ronald Reagan
burns the welfare checks with his
advanced radar guns, he may have
more internal wars in Harlem than
the entire Middle East. Perhaps
Ronnie will employ his B-52's to
stop the rioting rather than pro-
jecting them at Moscow.

Ronald Reagan will give the
moral majority an early Christmas
present as he appoints Archie
Bunker clones to the Supreme
Court. He may appoint the first
woman judge and she might even
be President of the Right to Life
Movement. Now that Ronnie's
judicial branch will be putting on
their white robes, and col-
laborating with Richard Nixon's
gang, Barbarino and Horshack
will have to learn how to read a bi-
ble
Ronald will make the U.S. feel
superior; even Ayatollah will feel a
bit inferior. With George as V.P.
hiding in the "bushes," he can con-
tact the CIA to send agent-orange
over to Afghanistan. The Pink
Panther will "anther" and the Per-
sian Gulf will fall under the
auspices of the President of Exxon.
No longer will Ayatollah
Khonehead permeate the macho
membrane of this great and
wondrous nation.
Since Birch has said "bye," and
South Dakota's liberal will not

dismount Teddy K. from his
judicial committee and place him
on the Lifeguard Regulatory Com-

and use them to furthur Standard

With Nancy and Ronald at the
White House, America will return
to the good old days of Herbert
Hoover. Mr. Reagan will be really
"laissez-faire" to corporate
business and let the minorities
justify their own end. As the
Statue of LIberty now embraces
its lighted torch, Ronnie will lower

of nuclear power plants. Anita
Bryant is a likely candidate to lead

Ronnie will swing the Pendulum

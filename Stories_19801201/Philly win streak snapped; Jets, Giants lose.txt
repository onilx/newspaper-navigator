Philly win streak snapped; Jets, Giants lose

SAN DIEGO (AP) - Dan
Fouts passed for two touchdowns
and 342 yards and Rolf Benirschke
booted field goals of 34, 45 and 42
yards as the San Diego Chargers
outlasted Philadelphia 22-21
yesterday, ending the Eagles'
eight-game National Football
League winning streak.
Trailing 19-0 at halftime,
Philadelphia roared back behind
quarterback Ron Jaworski, who
brought the Eagles to within one
point with 2:50 remaining as he hit
Wilbert Montgomery with an 11-
yard scoring pass.
The victory, San Diego's ninth
in 13 games, assured the Chargers
of at least a tie for the lead in the
AFC West. Philadelphia is 11-2
after the loss ended the club's
longest winning streak since 1960.
Fouts stunned the Eagles with a
game-opening 50-yard bomb to
John Jefferson, triggering a three-
play, 66-yard scoring drive that
ended with a 14-yard scoring toss
from Fouts to Kellen Winslow.
After Benirschke's 34-yard field
goal, Fouts hit Winslow with a 17-
yard touchdown pass. Benirschke's
second field goal, a 45-yarder,
came with one second remaining in
the opening half.
The Eagles marched 80 yards in
15 plays to open the second half,
with Montgomery capping the
march on a one-yard run.
Benirschke kicked the field goal
that turned out to be the game-
winner, a 42-yarder, early in the
fourth quarter. Jaworski hit Keith
Krepfle with a 16-yard scoring
pass to make it 22-14 with 11:07
remaining.
ANAHEIM, Calif. (AP) -
Quarterback Vince Ferragamo
threw four touchdown passes,
despite being intercepted four
times, and led the Los Angeles
Rams to a 38-13 National Foot-
ball League triumph over the New
York Jets yesterday.
The. Rams dominated from the
start and Ferragamo's scoring pas-
ses covered 12 yards to Cullen
Bryant, 44 to Billy Waddy and 15
and 19 to Willie Miller before a

crowd of 59,743 at Anaheim
Stadium.
The Rams, 9-4 and one game
behind Atlanta in the NFC West,
built a 10-0 first quarter lead on
the Ferragamo-Bryant pass and a
25-yard field goal by Frank Cor-
ral.

The Jets, 3-10, scored all their
points in the second quarter on
field goals of 22 and 40 by Pat
Leahy and a 35-yard pass from

Richard Todd to Johnny Jones.
EAST RUTHERFORD (AP)
Ottis Anderson ran for two
touchdowns and surpassed the
1,000-yard rushing mark as the St.
Louis Cardinals downed the New
York Giants 23-7 yesterday in a
National Football League contest.
Neil O'Donoghue accounted for
the Cardinals' other nine points
with three field goals.
The victory boosted the Car-
dinals' record to 4-9, while the

Giants fell to 3-10.
The second-year running back
carried the Cardinals through their
first scoring drive, running the ball
on nine of 12 plays which took
them 86 yards in the first quarter.
The touchdown came on a six-yard
dive.

O'Donoghue padded the Car-
dinal lead to 10-0 on a 43-yard
field goal midway through the
third period.

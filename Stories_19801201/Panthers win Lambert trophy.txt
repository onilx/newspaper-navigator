Panthers win Lambert trophy

NEW YORK (AP) - The
University of Pittsburgh won the
Lambert Trophy as the out-
standing major college football
team in the East for the second
year in a row, it was announced
yesterday.
The Panthers, who are ranked
fourth nationally, defeated fifth-
ranked Penn State 14-9 last Friday

to finish the regular season with a
10-1 record. Pitt received 70 points
from the eight-man Lambert panel
to 61 for Penn State, which had a
9-2 record.
Navy, 8-3, was third with 56
points after defeating Army 33-6.
The Midshipmen were followed by
Yale, 8-2, with 51 points and
Rutgers, 7-4, with 40.

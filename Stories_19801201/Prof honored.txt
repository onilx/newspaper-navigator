Prof honored

William Gillette, an associate
professor of history at Douglass
College, is this year's recipient of
the Chastain Award for his book,
Retreat from Reconstruction,
1869-1879, published this year by
the Louisiana State University
Press.
The Chastain Award is
presented annually by the
Southern Political Science As-
sociation to the author of the best
book on the politics of the South.
This is the second prize for Gillet-
te's book, which also won the
Landry Award.

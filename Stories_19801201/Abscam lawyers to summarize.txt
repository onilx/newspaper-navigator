Abscam lawyers to summarize

NEW YORK (AP) - The
federal Abscam bribery-
conspiracy trial of Reps. Frank
Thompson Jr. and John Murphy
resumes today with government
and defense lawyers summarizing
their cases for the jury.

U.S. District Judge George
Pratt was expected to question
members of the Brooklyn jury if
any were aware of last Wednes-
day's ruling by a judge in
Philadelphia throwing out the Ab-
scam convictions of two city coun-
cilmen there on entrapment
grounds. The jury of eight men and
four women in the trial of Murphy

(D-N.Y.) and Thompson (D-N.J.)
has not been sequestered but jurors
have been told not to monitor news
broadcasts or read newspapers.
Before federal prosecutor
Thomas Puccio makes his summa-
tion, the jury will hear brief
testimony by an FBI technician
about the authentcity of an audio
tape of a Oct. 20, 1979 meeting
where Murphy allegedly agreed to
be influenced on immigration mat-
ters in return for $5,000.

Last week, Puccio indicated that
his summation could take as long
as 21/2 hours with another 30
minutes reserved for rebuttal.

Stephen Kaufman, Thompson's
main attorney, said his closing
remarks could take as long as
three hours. Michael Tigar,
Murphy's attorney, said he would
speak no longer than 90 minutes.
Last week Tigar unexpectedly
rested his client's case without call-
ing one witness. He has since
refused to comment on the tactic,
explaining that he would make his
position clear in his closing state-
ment.
Attorneys for both sides in-
dicated last week they would
probably use portions of
videotapes played at the trial in
their closing presentations.
